Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 June 2026

This page is maintained by PropSite to explain how we collect, use and protect your data when you use propsite.in and our WhatsApp bot. It is informational and does not constitute an independent certification.

1. What we collect
Broker profile: name, phone, WhatsApp number, email, agency, RERA, avatar, logo.
Listing data: text descriptions, photos, videos and captions you upload or send via WhatsApp.
Buyer leads: name, phone and message that buyers submit on your listing pages.
Usage data: page views, clicks, device/browser type, IP address, approximate location.
2. How we use it
To create and host your public listing pages and broker profile.
To send you lead notifications and account / transactional messages.
To improve the product (aggregated analytics, debugging).
To comply with legal obligations.
3. What is public

Anything you publish as a "live" listing — headline, price, photos, locality, your broker name, agency and contact number — is publicly visible. Sensitive fields like email, raw WhatsApp notes and internal IDs are never exposed on the public listing page.

4. AI processing

We use third-party AI providers to convert your free-text WhatsApp notes into structured listing fields, descriptions and highlights. Only the text required for that task is sent.

5. Sub-processors
Lovable Cloud / Supabase — database, authentication, storage.
Bunny.net — media (image / video) hosting and CDN.
Meta / WhatsApp Business — inbound and outbound WhatsApp messages.
OpenRouter / Groq / Lovable AI Gateway — AI inference.
6. Retention

We retain account and listing data for as long as your account is active. You can delete individual listings at any time from your dashboard. Email us to delete your account and all associated personal data.

7. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored in databases protected by row-level security so brokers can only access their own listings and leads. Passwords are hashed by our auth provider. No system is 100% secure; we follow industry standards and patch issues promptly.

8. Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by emailing support@propsite.in. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Cookies

We use first-party cookies / local storage to keep you signed in and to remember preferences. We do not run third-party advertising trackers.
