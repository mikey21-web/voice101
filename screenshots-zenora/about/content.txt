About Zenvistas

Zenvistas is a haven of modern comfort and serene living, nestled in the peaceful surroundings of Veeriampalayam, Coimbatore. This exclusive community features 59 premium 4 BHK & 5 BHK villas, thoughtfully designed with north and south-facing orientations to capture natural light and ventilation. Each villa embodies refined architecture, spacious interiors, and a perfect blend of elegance and tranquility — offering you a lifestyle that is both luxurious and harmonious.
