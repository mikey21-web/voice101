CARD 1 — Title
VEZRAA
The AI Real Estate Operating System
More sales. Less effort. One platform that captures the lead, works it, books the site visit, and closes the loop.

---

CARD 2 — The problem you already pay for
You are not short on leads. You are short on site visits.
- You pay ₹1,500 to ₹4,000 per lead on 99acres, Housing, MagicBricks and Meta. Most of it never becomes a walk-in.
- Speed: a lead that waits 40 minutes has already spoken to your competitor.
- Qualification: your telecalling desk burns the day on buyers with no budget and no timeline.
- No-shows: a booked site visit with no reminder and no location pin is a coin flip.
- After hours: your buyers browse at 11pm on Sunday. Your office is shut.
Your CRM records every one of these failures. It prevents none of them.

---

CARD 3 — What Vezraa is
Not a CRM with a chatbot bolted on. An operating system for your sales floor.
- Captures every lead from website, ads, portals, WhatsApp, calls and walk-ins, with duplicate detection.
- Scores, qualifies and auto-assigns before a human touches it.
- Works the lead across WhatsApp, SMS, email and voice until it books.
- Runs the site visit end to end, from scheduling to GPS check-in.
- Manages the inventory, the booking, the paperwork and the channel partner behind it.

CARD 4 — Mikey, your autonomous AI sales rep
The part nobody else has. An AI rep that works your pipeline 24x7.
- Understands and nurtures every lead, not just the ones someone remembered to call.
- Multi-channel follow-up that does not stop after attempt two.
- Qualifies, prioritises and schedules appointments on its own.
- Escalates the hot leads to your closer the moment they turn hot.
- Learns from what actually converted and improves.

---

CARD 5 — AI voice agents that actually call
Your buyers pick up the phone. Vezraa is on the other end.
- Calls new leads instantly, in the buyer's language.
- Natural conversation, not an IVR tree.
- Answers project FAQs, books site visits, updates the CRM by itself.
- Never misses a lead at midnight, on a Sunday, or during a launch weekend rush.
- Every call recorded, logged and searchable.

---

CARD 6 — Site visits, controlled end to end
This is where your revenue is won or lost. It gets its own system.
- Schedule the visit, confirm it, remind before it.
- GPS check-in and check-out, so you know who actually showed.
- OTP verification on the visit, so the attribution is real.
- Visit feedback captured while the buyer is still on site.
- Executive performance measured on visits conducted, not calls dialled.

---

CARD 7 — Immersive experiences that shorten the decision
Buyers decide faster when they can see it.
- Interactive 3D tours and AR/VR walkthroughs.
- WebVerse project showrooms for buyers who cannot travel.
- Real-time experience sharing with the sales executive guiding the buyer.
- One link that carries photos, videos, the 3D tour, the brochure and the enquiry form.
- Share by link or QR from any listing, hoarding or ad.

One vendor. One login. One source of truth.
- CRM and pipeline: visual stages, opportunities, tasks, 360 degree customer view.
- Property management: projects, towers, floors, units, live availability, pricing, payment plans, hold and book.
- Channel partners: onboarding, inventory access, bookings, payouts and commissions, partner portal.
- Flow builder and marketing automation: drag and drop workflows, drip campaigns, approvals, escalations.

CARD 9 — Built for Indian real estate, not adapted to it
- RERA registration number carried automatically in every project-specific message.
- DPDP compliant: explicit opt-in captured before any property updates go out.
- WhatsApp Business API first, because that is where your buyers already are.
